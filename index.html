Wish Fork

a song that keeps deciding where to go

Roughly twice a minute the piece reaches a fork. If you choose, it follows you. If you drift, it reads the hour, the cold, and your stillness — and wishes for you. Eleven forks, about three and a half minutes, every journey shareable.
